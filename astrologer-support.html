Contact us

Email support.astroshrine@gmail.com with your registered phone number and a short description of the issue (screenshots help). We reply within 24–48 hours.

Common questions

How do I join as an astrologer?

Fill the astrologer onboarding form. After our team verifies your details you can sign in to the Astrologer app with the same phone number.

I can't sign in

Use the phone number or Google account you registered with. If the OTP doesn't arrive, wait a minute and tap resend. If your account is not yet approved, contact us.

How do I go online and receive chats or calls?

Turn the Online switch on from the home screen and allow notifications so you are alerted to new requests. Requests not answered within 3 minutes count as missed.

How do I set my schedule and bookings?

Open My Schedule & Bookings on the home screen to set your available hours and see upcoming appointments.

When and how are earnings paid?

See the Astrologer Payout Policy for payout timing, TDS and the bank details we need.

How do I delete my account?

See Delete Account, or email us from your registered email.
