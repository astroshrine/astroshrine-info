1. Introduction

AstroShrine Private Limited ("AstroShrine", "we", "us") operates the AstroShrine Astrologer app (the "Astrologer App"), used by astrologers and consultants who offer consultations to AstroShrine users. This policy explains what we collect from astrologers, why, and how it is protected. Customers of the AstroShrine app are covered by our main Privacy Policy. The Astrologer App is for adults (18+) only.

2. Information We Collect

a) Information you provide

DataPurpose
Name, phone number, emailAccount sign-in (OTP or Google Sign-In) and contacting you
Profile photo, bio, expertise, languages, experienceYour public astrologer profile shown to users

b) Information collected automatically

DataPurpose

Device identifiers and push notification tokensAlerting you to new chat and call requests
IP address, device type and OS, app usageSecurity, fraud prevention and improving the app

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Creating and showing your astrologer profile to users
  • Connecting you with users for chat, call and booked consultations
  • Verifying your identity and meeting tax and legal obligations
  • Calculating and paying your earnings
  • Sending notifications about requests, bookings and payouts
  • Quality, safety and fraud checks, and resolving complaints

4. What Users Can See

Users see your public profile: display name, photo, bio, expertise, languages, experience, ratings, reviews and online status, plus the messages and media you send them during a consultation. Users never see your phone number, email, address, identity documents, PAN, bank details or earnings.

During a consultation you can see the user's display name and the birth details they share for the reading. You must use them only for that consultation and must not contact users outside AstroShrine.

5. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with providers that help us run the service:

6. Storage and Security

  • Data is stored on secure cloud infrastructure and encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Identity documents and bank details are accessible only to authorised AstroShrine staff for verification and payouts; admin access is logged
  • The full Aadhaar number is not shown in the app or to users

7. Data Retention

  • Profile and account data: while your account is active
  • Consultation, earnings, payout and tax records: as long as required by law (generally up to 8 years)
  • Identity documents: while you are an active astrologer and as long as the law requires afterwards
  • On account deletion, other personal data is removed within 30 days (see Data Deletion)

8. Your Rights

  • Access and correct your profile in the app, or ask us to correct other data
  • Delete your account (see Data Deletion); records the law requires us to keep are retained
  • Withdraw consent for notifications in your phone settings
  • Raise a concern by emailing us at the address below; we reply within 30 days

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
