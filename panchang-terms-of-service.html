4. Calculations and Conventions

Calculations use Swiss Ephemeris-based astronomy and classical rules. The conventions used (Lahiri/Chitrapaksha ayanamsa by default, sunrise convention, amanta or purnimanta months, regional festival variants) are shown in Settings. Different almanacs follow different conventions, so timings may differ by a few minutes — or, rarely, a festival by a day — from other panchangs.
