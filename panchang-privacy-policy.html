1. Introduction

AstroShrine Private Limited ("AstroShrine", "we", "us"), Raipur, Chhattisgarh (DPIIT DIPP284892), is the data fiduciary for the AstroShrine Kundli app ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains what personal data the App processes, why, and your rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. It covers the Panchang app only; the main AstroShrine app has its own Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

DataPurpose
Mobile numberAccount creation and OTP login. No promotional calls or SMS
Profile: name, gender; optional birth date, time and placePersonalising the App; "self" in the muhurat finder
Kundlis, matches and "Meri Tithi" entriesShowing your charts and reminders; syncing them to your account
Your answer to "Do you know jyotish?"Choosing how detailed the App is

Information collected automatically

DataPurpose
Location (chosen city, or GPS latitude/longitude if you allow it)Calculating sunrise, Rahu kaal and other local timings — sent per request, not stored with any identifier
Birth details for a kundli or matchComputing the chart on our server — not stored there afterwards
Usage statistics (random install ID, screen and event names) — only with your consentImproving the App. No IP address, name, phone or birth details
Push tokenFestival and vrat notifications you have enabled

Information from third parties

None. We do not buy or receive personal data about you from anyone.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and secure your account
  • To calculate and display the panchang, muhurat, kundli and matching results
  • To sync your saved data across your devices
  • To send reminders and notifications you have enabled
  • To improve the App using consented, anonymous statistics
  • To comply with law

We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell it.

4. Sharing of Information

We share data only with service providers who process it on our behalf: Supabase (account and synced data), Railway (calculation server), Firebase Cloud Messaging (notifications), MSG91 (OTP SMS) and OpenStreetMap/Nominatim (the city name you type in place search, without any identifier). We may disclose data if required by law. We never share your data with astrologers, advertisers or data brokers.

5. Data Storage and Security

  • Saved kundlis are encrypted on your phone with XChaCha20-Poly1305; the key is held in the Android Keystore / iOS Keychain
  • All network traffic uses HTTPS
  • Synced data is protected by row-level security — only your account can read it

6. Data Retention

DataKept for

Push tokensUntil notifications are turned off or the token becomes invalid
Location and birth details sent for calculationsNot retained after the calculation

7. Your Rights

Under the DPDP Act, 2023 you may access, correct and erase your personal data, withdraw consent, nominate another person to exercise these rights, and raise a grievance.

  • Correct your profile: Settings → My account
  • Withdraw usage-statistics consent: Settings → Privacy
  • Delete your account and all synced data: Settings → My account → "Delete Panchang data"
  • Data kept only on the phone is erased by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their data. A kundli entered for a child is entered by, and under the control of, a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy. Material changes will be shown in the App for a fresh acceptance and the "Last updated" date above will change.
