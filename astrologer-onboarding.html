3. NATURE OF ENGAGEMENT

3.1 The Astrologer is an independent consultant, not an employee, partner or agent. No employer–employee relationship is created. Taxes other than TDS deducted by the Company are the Astrologer’s responsibility.

3.2 Services are astrology consultation (birth chart, predictions, remedies, compatibility, career and related guidance) by chat, audio or video, as enabled on the Platform.

3.3 The Astrologer shall keep professional conduct, shall not guarantee a specific outcome, and shall comply with applicable law. Consultations are opinion and guidance, not legal, medical or financial advice.

4. COMMERCIAL TERMS, GST, OFFERS AND HOURS

4.1 Fee. The Company pays the fee or revenue share shown on the Dashboard (commission, per-minute or package). The rate displayed and accepted for a consultation is the rate for that consultation.

4.2 Cycle. Payout is monthly, on or before the 10th of the following month, for completed consultations not reversed, after TDS and any Platform deduction, and only after KYC and bank details are verified. A statement is available on the Dashboard.

4.3 GST. Company GSTIN is 22AAZCA9085B1ZE. If the Astrologer is GST-registered, they shall quote their GSTIN and raise a tax invoice as applicable. If they are not registered, payout is without GST charged by them. The Company may deduct TDS under the Income-tax Act at the rate applicable to the payment. GST on the user’s consultation fee, where charged by the Platform, is the Company’s compliance, and does not change the Astrologer’s share shown on the Dashboard.

4.4 Bank. Payout goes only to a bank account or UPI in the Astrologer’s own name. The Company is not liable for failure caused by wrong details.

4.5 Offers. The Astrologer will follow offers, schemes, campaigns and special rates the Company puts on the Platform, where the rate or share is shown on the Dashboard before the Astrologer goes online for that offer. Refusal without prior written approval may be treated as a breach and may lead to suspension or termination.

4.6 Hours. Minimum availability is 6 hours a day:

  • at least 3 hours between 6:00 AM and 6:00 PM; and
  • at least 3 hours between 6:00 PM and 6:00 AM.

Missing this minimum without prior Dashboard or written approval may be treated as a breach.

5. OBLIGATIONS OF THE ASTROLOGER

5.1 Keep personal, professional and KYC details, including the current photograph, accurate and updated.

5.2 Keep user data confidential and not misuse it.

5.3 Not engage in fraud, misleading claims or illegal activity.

5.4 Follow the Platform code of conduct and quality guidelines.

5.5 Not solicit users off the Platform, and not share personal contact details for offline work, without prior written approval.

5.6 Participate in displayed offers as in Clause 4.5, and keep the hours in Clause 4.6.

5.7 Not record a consultation, or use user data for any other purpose, unless the Platform feature and the user both allow it.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY AND DATA

6.1 User information, Platform data and commercial terms are confidential, and shall not be disclosed without the Company’s prior written consent, except as required by law.

6.2 The Astrologer consents to collection and processing of personal data (masked Aadhaar, PAN, GSTIN if any, photograph, address, bank details) for onboarding, verification, payment and legal compliance only.

7. TERM AND TERMINATION

7.1 This Agreement starts on OTP verification and/or signature, and continues until terminated.

7.2 Either party may terminate by 15 days’ prior written notice, or by notice on the Dashboard.

7.3 The Company may suspend or end access immediately, without liability for future fees, for breach, fraud, policy violation, refusal of a displayed offer, or failure of required availability. Fees already accrued for completed, non-reversed consultations stay payable in the next cycle, subject to lawful set-off.
