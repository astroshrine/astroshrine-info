1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the AstroShrine Compass Android app (package com.astroshrine.compass, the "App") handles information. The App is published by AstroShrine Private Limited, Raipur, Chhattisgarh, India ("AstroShrine", "we"). It is written to meet the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and Google Play's User Data policy.

2. In short

  • The App works fully offline. Its release version has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything to AstroShrine or anyone else.
  • We do not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • No account or login is needed.
  • Everything you save (readings, plan checks, MahaVastu projects) stays on your phone until you delete it or uninstall the App.

3. What the App uses, and why

  • Motion and magnetic sensors (magnetometer, accelerometer, gyroscope / rotation vector): to work out which way the phone is pointing. Readings are used live on the phone and are not saved unless you tap Save.
  • Location (optional): only if you turn on True North or GPS. It is used on your phone to calculate the magnetic declination for your place and to show your coordinates. If you saved a reading while GPS was on, its coordinates are stored with that reading on your phone. Location is never sent anywhere.
  • Floor-plan photos and PDFs (optional): when you use the plan check or the MahaVastu Analyser, the image you choose is read on your phone to draw the Vastu chakra over it.
  • Labels and notes you type (for example "Main door", a client name in a MahaVastu project): stored on your phone with the reading or project.

4. Permissions

  • Location (approximate and precise): optional, asked only when you turn on True North or GPS. If you refuse, the App keeps working on Magnetic North.
  • Photos: the App uses Android's system photo picker, which gives it only the one photo you pick. It does not ask for storage or media permission.
  • Camera: when you choose "Take photo", Android's own camera app takes the picture and hands it to the App. The App itself does not ask for camera permission.
  • PDF files: opened through Android's document picker, which gives access only to the file you choose.

You can withdraw any permission at any time in Android Settings → Apps → AstroShrine Compass → Permissions.

5. Sharing

The App shares nothing by itself. When you tap Share (a reading, a plan-check result) or save a MahaVastu PDF report, Android's share or print sheet opens and the text or file goes only to the app or printer you choose. How that app treats it is governed by its own policy.

6. Storage and Deletion

Saved readings and settings are kept in the App's private storage on your phone. MahaVastu projects are kept in the App's private web storage on your phone. Plan images picked for a check are temporary copies in the App's cache.

To delete: remove a reading in History, delete a project inside the MahaVastu Analyser, or clear everything with Android Settings → Apps → AstroShrine Compass → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling the App. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

The App turns off Android's automatic backup, so its data is not copied to your Google account.

7. Children's Privacy

The App collects no personal data from anyone, including children. It is intended for general audiences; we recommend that children use the Vastu features with a parent or guardian.

8. Vastu guidance

Directions, zones, verdicts and remedies shown by the App are traditional Vastu guidance, not engineering, legal, medical or financial advice. Accuracy depends on your phone's sensors, calibration and nearby metal or electronics. Do not make structural changes without a qualified professional.

9. Changes to This Policy

If the App ever starts to collect data (for example an online feature), we will update this Policy before that version is released and change the "Last updated" date above.
