2. The App

The App is a free, offline digital compass. It shows the direction your phone is pointing (Magnetic or True North), the MahaVastu zones and directions with their traditional meanings, a Vastu check of a floor plan you provide, and the MahaVastu Analyser (45-devta Vastu Purusha Mandala) with a printable report. No account is needed. There are no ads and no in-app purchases.

3. Compass Accuracy

  • Readings come from your phone's sensors and can be affected by calibration, nearby metal, magnets, electrical wiring, appliances, vehicles and the phone's case or cover.
  • Follow the App's calibration and "move away from metal" prompts, keep the phone flat, and confirm important readings at more than one spot.
  • Do not use the App for navigation, aviation, marine, mining, surveying, safety-critical or emergency purposes. It is not a certified instrument.
  • True North uses a standard geomagnetic model for your location and today's date; local magnetic anomalies are not included.
